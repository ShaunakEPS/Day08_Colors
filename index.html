Colorful Website: Analogous

The color scheme I am using for this is an analogous color scheme that consists of shades of red. The darkest red used in the webpage is for the background, and this color is used because dark shades of red are proven to raise blood pressure and bring on feelings of anger. This website uses many colors that are similar, but they are also slightly different, and the slight, minute differences go well with the elicited feeling of anger that this website aims to create. Overall, the website evokes a feeling of anger and raises blood pressure by using red and a palette with hard-to-discern colors.
